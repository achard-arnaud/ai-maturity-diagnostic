# Research and evidence OS

## Source order

1. Primary corporate, regulatory, legal, financial, technical, career, and product sources.
2. Primary external sources such as regulators, procurement records, and named partner material.
3. Reliable specialist and financial press for corroboration or challenge.
4. Community, employee, customer, and aggregator sources as directional evidence only.

## Atomic claim model

Capture a stable claim ID, source ID, source type, URL or path, publication date, event date, observation date, consultation date, precise support, epistemic status, evidence grade, confidence, contradiction links, and decision relevance.

Use:

- `fact | inference | hypothesis | unknown`;
- `P1` direct primary evidence;
- `P2` partial primary evidence requiring reconstruction;
- `U1` owner or user-provided information;
- `W1` secondary web evidence;
- `N0` unsupported or unknown.

Never upgrade a grade without stronger evidence. Preserve counter-evidence and access limitations.

## Triangulation and inference

Use one strong primary signal or two independent signals for a material capability gap. Infer from clusters, sequences, and co-occurrence rather than isolated keywords. State a competing explanation and the observation that would distinguish it.

## Time discipline

Keep announcement, event, launch, deployment, result, and consultation dates separate. For hiring, default to six months of priority evidence plus six months of context unless the sector requires another window.

## Fail closed

Write `Non établi` when support is insufficient. Keep an unnamed function rather than substituting a neighboring title. Do not transform communication volume, a job posting, an acquisition, a partnership, or an isolated use case into proof of strategy, budget, adoption, or scale.

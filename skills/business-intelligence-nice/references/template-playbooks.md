# Template playbooks

## Selection

| Output | Pages | Use when | Functional spine |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Executive one-pager | 1 | One decision, narrow evidence set | answer, three proofs, risk, action |
| Executive two-pager | 2 | Decision plus due diligence | answer, mechanism, benchmark, gates, action |
| Entity two-pager | 2 | Meeting prep on company + person + named offerings/IP | company atom, person atom, page-2 material product spotlight, strategic read, discussion angles |
| Benchmark | 2–4 | Several comparable options | criteria, gates, matrix, sensitivity, implication |
| Business note | 5–7 | Context and evidence need room | answer, context, evidence, analysis, options, action |
| Transformation diagnostic | 5–7 | Multi-lane operating question | baseline, work reality, capability, governance, response, roadmap |
| Pilot brief | 2–4 | Fit already validated | hypothesis, baseline, design, guardrails, scale/iterate/stop |

## Business note 5–7 pages

1. Executive answer and decision requested.
2. Context, scope, baseline, and economic or professional problem.
3. Evidence and contradictions.
4. Analysis of the decisive mechanisms.
5. Options, alternatives, exclusions, and trade-offs.
6. Recommendation, implementation conditions, and risks.
7. Optional appendix limited to sources, definitions, or gates that materially support the decision.

## Benchmark

Define comparable units before scoring. Apply hard gates first. Use three to seven criteria that correspond to the decision, not to vendor marketing. Preserve missing data. Include one credible alternative and one sensitivity test. End with the evidence needed to change the ranking.

## Rendering handoff

Map the approved content to a Nice Output Engine template only after the page-level purpose is stable. Each page must have one governing question or decision. A visual block may summarize analysis but may not create it.

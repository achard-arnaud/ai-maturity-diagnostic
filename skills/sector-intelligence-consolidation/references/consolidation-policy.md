# Sector consolidation policy

## Eligibility

Require:

- one defensible ICB sector code;
- at least three account studies;
- studies no older than six months by default;
- non-empty evidence claims and capability gaps;
- profile confidence `medium` or `high`.

Candidate ICB mappings (`N0`) support exploratory grouping only. A rollup becomes `decision_grade` only when every covered account has a validated ICB mapping. Preserve each account's mapping ID, status, and confidence in the rollup.

## Synthesis

For every recurring theme, retain account count, company IDs, claim IDs, epistemic status, counterexamples, and confidence.

Do not:

- infer prevalence from three conveniently available accounts;
- combine public bodies with equity ICB companies;
- merge different ICB sectors for narrative convenience;
- expose private people or infer sector demand from network density;
- promote repeated hypotheses to facts.

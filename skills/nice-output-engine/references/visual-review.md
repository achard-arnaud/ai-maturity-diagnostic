# Visual review

Inspect every rendered page at 100% and 150%.

1. Confirm the decision or governing question is visible within five seconds.
2. Confirm hierarchy separates conclusion, evidence, interpretation, and detail.
3. Confirm no text needs zooming and no density problem is hidden by small type.
4. Confirm cards align, gutters are stable, and no isolated block creates accidental emphasis.
5. Confirm semantic colors describe evidence or decision state rather than decoration.
6. Confirm tables are readable across columns and rows.
7. Confirm the last page ends with an action, implication, or explicit open decision.

Record concrete corrections, rebuild, and close the review only after the QA report passes.

# Pilot policy

Define:

- one representative workflow with explicit boundaries;
- a baseline period or reconstruction method;
- one primary business or flow KPI;
- quality, supervision, security, and full-cost guardrails;
- intervention and comparison design where practical;
- named sponsor and terrain owner;
- review cadence and escalation path;
- stop conditions and a scale decision.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Prefer a real production-like workflow with a baseline.
2. Use historical replay on representative cases when production access is premature.
3. Use a controlled benchmark with representative artifacts for bounded evidence.
4. Treat a synthetic demo only as technical feasibility evidence, never as ROI proof.

## Decision integrity

Design the proof to falsify as well as support the hypothesis. Include representative cases, failure handling, and full supervision or rework cost. Do not invent duration, pricing, savings, access, or staffing; mark them as discovery inputs when unknown.

# Contact-angle policy

Build the contact angle from:

```text
verified priority
+ observed bottleneck or gap
+ relevant proof from the selected offer
+ one validation question
```

Avoid feature-led outreach and unsupported personalization. Preserve the confidence of the underlying claims.

For Astraforge, prefer language about flow acceleration, quality or remediation, and controlled execution. Avoid leading with “agent platform,” “AI-native transformation,” or generic productivity.

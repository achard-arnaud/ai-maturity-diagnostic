# Research lenses

## 1. Corporate strategy

Look for funded priorities, operating targets, transformation programs, product or geographic bets, efficiency programs, the role assigned to AI, partnerships, and constraints.

Treat budget, a named sponsor, a timeline, a delivery program, a KPI, or repeated executive commitment as stronger signals than communication volume.

## 2. Organization and power

Map official reporting lines separately from probable influence. Distinguish:

- economic sponsor;
- technical sponsor;
- terrain owner;
- procurement, security, legal, or regulatory veto;
- executive narrative owner.

## 3. Hiring and capability signals

Use hiring to infer what the firm may be building, centralizing, outsourcing, or missing. Track recurrence, seniority, location, stack, reporting line, and intensity. Do not treat a posting as proof that a capability has been hired or deployed.

## 4. Newsflow and momentum

Classify events as launch, partnership, acquisition, incident, regulation, client pressure, financing, leadership change, or restructuring.

Retain a signal when it changes priority, urgency, budget, ownership, risk, or build-buy-partner posture.

## Capability-gap test

State each gap as:

```text
desired business state
- observable current capability
= missing or constrained capability
```

Avoid generic claims such as “needs more AI maturity.” Record the evidence, competing explanation, confidence, and question that would validate or refute the gap.

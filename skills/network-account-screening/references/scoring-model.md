# Network screening model v0.1

| Component | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Network access | 20 |
| Executive access signal | 25 |
| AI and data proximity | 20 |
| Delivery and transformation proximity | 15 |
| Buying-committee role coverage | 15 |
| ICB classification readiness | 5 |

## Tiers

- `A` ≥ 65: high research priority;
- `B` 45–64: medium research priority;
- `C` 25–44: low research priority;
- `D` < 25: hold.

Theoretical ICP tier means “worth researching given current access,” never “likely buyer.” Only enterprise research may establish demand, and only the matcher may establish product fit.

# Assembly policy

## Principle

Canonical atoms are stable research objects. The executive brief is a derived view.

The assembler may select, order and compress claims. It may not strengthen, invent, merge or silently drop a material entity.

## Page contract

The default Nice deliverable is exactly two pages.

### Page 1

Required blocks:

- `executive_snapshot`;
- `company_profile`;
- `person_profile`;
- `strategic_intersection`.

### Page 2

Required blocks:

- `product_spotlights`;
- `current_dynamics`;
- `strengths_and_differentiators`;
- `challenge_points`;
- `audience_fit`;
- `discussion_angles`;
- `takeaway`;
- `limitations`.

## Product retention rule

For every referenced external-product atom whose materiality is `core` or `material`, page 2 must contain one `product_spotlight` entry referencing that atom ID.

Supporting/incidental products can be omitted from the rendered brief without being deleted from canonical research.

## Persona isolation

The assembly can target `francois-pro`, `sarah-pro` or `neutral`.

Persona-specific interpretation may alter:

- relevance;
- comparison lens;
- discussion questions;
- likely synergies.

It must not alter:

- target-company facts;
- target-product facts;
- target-person facts;
- evidence grade or epistemic status.

## Compression priority

When the page is too dense, remove in this order:

1. repeated context;
2. low-materiality supporting products;
3. redundant examples;
4. secondary chronology.

Never remove:

- executive thesis;
- material product spotlight;
- decisive proof gap;
- person/company distinction;
- explicit uncertainty;
- final discussion angle.

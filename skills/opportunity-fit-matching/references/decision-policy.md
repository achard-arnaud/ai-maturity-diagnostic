# Decision policy

## PURSUE

Use only when the problem and major gates are demonstrated, the score is strong, and a credible proof path exists.

## VALIDATE

Use when fit is plausible but material uncertainties must be resolved before a firm pilot.

## NURTURE

Use when the problem may fit but timing, ownership, budget window, or readiness is not actionable.

## DISQUALIFY

Use when the problem is absent, the offer is structurally incompatible, a blocker fails, or another category clearly solves the need better.

## Required falsifier

For every `PURSUE` or `VALIDATE` result, complete:

> We would downgrade or disqualify this fit if [specific observable condition].

Preserve contradictions. Never average them away. Reduce evidence confidence when incompatible explanations remain live.

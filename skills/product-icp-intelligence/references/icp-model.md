# Product-side ICP model

Evaluate each offer across seven independent dimensions:

1. **Problem** — Verify that the target problem is materially present.
2. **Maturity** — Verify readiness for this solution category.
3. **Workflow** — Identify a repeatable workflow or unit of value.
4. **Technical** — Verify access to systems, data, tools, and controls.
5. **Organization** — Verify sponsor, owner, users, and operating-model compatibility.
6. **Economics** — Establish whether value and full cost can be measured.
7. **Timing** — Identify a trigger and decision window.

## Anti-ICP

Define conditions that make an offer structurally unsuitable or premature. Do not express anti-ICP as a low weighted score.

## Hard gates

Make each hard gate binary or ternary: `PASS | OPEN | FAIL`. Define its severity and observable test. Never allow a weighted score to compensate for a failed blocker.

## Product profile minimum

Include:

- canonical problem and anti-problem;
- outcomes and proof status;
- personas and ICP signals;
- anti-ICP;
- capabilities with claim strength;
- hard gates;
- canonical pilot pattern;
- alternatives and unknowns.

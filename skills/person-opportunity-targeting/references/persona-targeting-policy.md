# Persona targeting policy

## Sequence

Require this order:

```text
company evidence -> demand profile -> product fit -> person targeting
```

Never use a contact’s title to justify product fit.

## Interpretation

- A persona match is a hypothesis about relevance.
- An economic-sponsor label does not prove budget ownership.
- A terrain-owner label does not prove workflow ownership.
- A veto label does not prove formal approval rights.
- An undated role must remain `unverified` and lower confidence.

Validate current role, mandate, problem exposure, decision influence, and contact appropriateness before producing outreach.

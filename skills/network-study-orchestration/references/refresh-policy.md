# Refresh policy

Persist stable identities and taxonomy decisions. Refresh dynamic strategy, maturity, newsflow, roles, product matches, and reach hypotheses.

Default stale horizon: six months.

Queue a refresh when:

- a new contact batch creates or changes a relationship for the company;
- the newest study predates the company network update;
- the study exceeds the stale horizon;
- explicit contradictory evidence invalidates a current claim.

Creating a study is not completing research. Keep initialized studies at `enterprise_research` until `enterprise-demand-intelligence` produces evidence claims.

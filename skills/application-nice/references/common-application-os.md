# Common application OS

## Intake

Capture company, legal employer, ownership context, role title, location, reporting line, language, stage, deadline, channel, source job description, recruiter information, and requested artifacts. Keep employer and shareholder separate.

## Company intelligence

Use only the relevant business-intelligence lanes:

- corporate priorities and economic drivers;
- organization, decision rights, and influence;
- hiring signals and visible operating model;
- recent newsflow, appointments, partnerships, incidents, and momentum;
- enterprise demand, constraints, and capability gaps.

Separate fact, inference, hypothesis, and unknown. Research freshness must match the hiring decision date.

## Role success profile

Translate the job description into:

1. problems to solve;
2. outcomes at 6, 12, and 24 months when defensible;
3. decision rights and resources;
4. sponsor, terrain, peers, and veto players;
5. operating conditions and transformation stage;
6. explicit requirements and hidden tests;
7. likely competing candidate archetypes;
8. evidence that would disqualify the opportunity.

Do not confuse a long responsibility list with the actual mandate.

## Candidate-role matching

Apply knockout gates before scoring. Map each desired outcome to verified candidate evidence and label it `proven | adjacent | gap | unknown`. Compare at least one credible competing archetype. State what evidence would downgrade the fit.

Use:

- `PURSUE` — mandate and gates are strong enough for active application;
- `VALIDATE` — plausible fit but one material uncertainty needs resolution;
- `NURTURE` — fit may exist but timing or access is weak;
- `DECLINE` — structural mismatch, unattractive mandate, or failed critical gate.

## Narrative and assets

Build an executive through-line from the candidate's progression, motives, evidence, and relevance to the mandate. Select only the examples that prove the role's outcomes. Preserve the source CV and create a named variant for a specific opportunity.

## Interview system

Prepare:

- a 90-second and 3-minute narrative;
- five outcome stories with ownership, action, result, and limitation;
- likely objections and direct answers;
- questions testing mandate, governance, budget, team, data, technology, adoption, and value;
- a first-90-day hypothesis clearly marked as provisional;
- decision criteria for the candidate, not only the employer.

## Submission gate

Check names, dates, metrics, language, filenames, attachments, links, audience, and requested format. Ensure no fact from the inactive persona appears anywhere in the package.
